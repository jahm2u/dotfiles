## Key Insights

### Company/Team Detection Heuristics:
1. **Attendee count:**
   - 2 people = Likely 1-on-1
   - 3-5 people = Small team meeting
   - 6-10 people = Team meeting
   - 10+ people = Company-wide meeting

2. **Email domains:**
   - All @ipmedia.com.br = IPMedia internal
   - Mix @ipmedia + @meupatrocinio = Meu Patrocinio team
   - External domains (@gmail, @chili.pa, etc.) = External/partner meeting

3. **Keywords needing team/company patterns:**
   - "Team", "Weekly", "Standup", "Headquarters", "Overview", "KPI"
   - These should NOT default to 1-on-1

4. **Projects/Products as identifiers:**
   - "MP" (Meu Patrocinio), "MeuMatch", "Gone" = Product-specific meetings
